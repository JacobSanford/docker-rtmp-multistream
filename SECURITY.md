# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Email [jacob.josh.sanford@gmail.com](mailto:jacob.josh.sanford@gmail.com). Do not open a public issue.

Include the image tag or commit, the settings involved (with stream keys removed), and the steps that show the problem.

## Supported Versions

The `1.x` branch is the maintained branch. Fixes land there.

## Scope

For what the relay protects and what it leaves open by design, such as open playback on port 1935, see [docs/security.md](docs/security.md).
